## 1. Executive Summary

We performed a full source audit of the `AirControl` (`:app` and `:gesture-engine`) codebase, focusing on the recent low-latency/production commits as well as the underlying camera, MediaPipe hand/face inference, gesture state machine, and accessibility cursor pipelines.

### Root Causes Identified & Fixed

| ID | Subsystem | Root Cause Found in Source Audit | Production Fix Implemented |
|---|---|---|---|
| **RC-1** | **Hand Tracking (`HandTracker.kt` / `HandFrame.kt`)** | Although `HandLandmarker` was configured with `setNumHands(2)` and `TemporalHandTracker` existing in the repo was unused, `HandTrackerImpl.onLiveStreamResult` called `selectHandIndex(result)` and discarded the second detected hand. `HandFrame` could only represent a single hand (`landmarks: List<NormalizedLandmark>`). | Introduced `TrackedHand` (`trackId`, `landmarks`, `handedness`, `confidence`, `timestampMs`, `frameAspectRatio`, `landmarkQuality`, `isAmbiguous`) and `MultiHandAssociator` supporting up to 2 simultaneous hands with temporal velocity prediction, 2×2 minimum-cost assignment, duplicate-detection suppression, hand-crossing/overlap ambiguity flagging, and single-hand handedness hysteresis. |

## 2. Architecture & Pipeline Verification

### 2.1 Simultaneous Dual-Hand + Binocular Gaze Pipeline
- **Shared Zero-Allocation Frame Lease (`FrameOwnership` + `ReusableBitmapPool`):** Each camera frame is converted once on `analysisExecutor` into a pooled `ARGB_8888` bitmap using a cached `Matrix` and cached `Canvas`, wrapped in a shared `MPImage`, and leased to both `HandTracker` and `FaceTracker` with reference-counted completion (`consumerCount = 2` when eye tracking is active).
- **Dual-Hand Association (`MultiHandAssociator`):** Associates up to 2 MediaPipe hand detections per frame against active tracks using palm/wrist kinematics, preserves per-hand `trackId`, handedness, confidence, and landmark quality, suppresses duplicate overlapping detections of the same physical hand, and flags crossing/overlapping hands (`isAmbiguous = true`) so discrete actions cannot misfire during occlusion.
- **Dual-Hand Gesture Engine (`GestureEngine`):** Runs independent state machines per `trackId` (`ARMING`, `ARMED`, `EXECUTING`, `COOLDOWN`), locks pointer ownership to the pinching hand during `PinchPhase.START`/`MOVE`, and enforces a `180ms` cross-hand arbitration window so simultaneous two-hand gestures cannot double-fire system actions.
- **Binocular Eye Gaze (`FaceTrackerImpl`):** Extracts left and right iris centers (`468..472`, `473..477`) inside rotation-aligned eye local frames (`eyeCornerAngleRad`), fuses binocular eye ratios with per-eye quality weights, checks binocular consistency (`binocularDisagreement`), compensates head pose yaw/pitch/roll + IPD proxy scale, and gates blink/dwell clicks on `isActionable`.

## 3. What Was Verified Automatedly vs. What Requires Physical Hardware (`SM-X216B`)

### Proven by Automated Compilation & JVM/Robolectric Tests
- Full `:app` and `:gesture-engine` compilation (`compile_applet`).
- Dual-hand association (`MultiHandAssociatorTest`): simultaneous 2-hand 21-landmark preservation, `trackId` persistence across reordered MediaPipe outputs, single-hand dropout continuity, hand-crossing ambiguity flagging, and handedness flip hysteresis.
- Dual-hand gesture state isolation (`DualHandGestureEngineTest`): independent per-hand state machines, secondary hand dropout without disarming primary hand, and ambiguous-hand pinch suppression.
- Low-latency filter & telemetry truthfulness (`LowLatencyAndTelemetryAuditTest`): velocity reset across `> 120ms` frame gaps, confidence-scaled velocity lead, exact 2D Euclidean pixel + visual-angle error calculation, and independent `handResultFps`/`faceResultFps`/`cursorDispatchAgeP50Ms` telemetry.

### Requires Physical Tablet Verification (`Samsung Galaxy Tab A9+ 5G / SM-X216B`)
- Actual front-camera sensor AE FPS range behavior in indoor vs. bright lighting (most tablet front sensors cap exposure at 30 FPS indoors even when higher ranges are requested).
- Real Qualcomm Adreno GPU delegate initialization latency vs. CPU XNNPACK inference duration on `SM-X216B` under sustained 15-minute thermal load.
- Physical optical-to-display photon-to-photon latency (which requires an external high-speed camera pointed at both the user's hand and the tablet display; in-app telemetry measures `Frame→Cursor` pipeline age from `SystemClock.elapsedRealtime()` at analyzer entry to `AccessibilityService` cursor dispatch).
